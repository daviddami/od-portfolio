Off duty

Hobbies & interests

Things I do when I am not studying or on an event crew.

  • Travelling

    Seeing new places, usually with a camera. Trips double as photography projects.

  • Gaming

    Strategy and story-driven games. Good practice in problem-solving and patience.

  • Movies

    Watching for cinematography as much as story — it feeds directly into my videography.

  • Music

    A constant companion while editing photos and writing code.

  • Research

    Reading up on new hardware, networking and creative tools, then trying them out.

  • Volunteering

    Community work with TEDx Samaru, Enactus ABU and KAFART.

Behind the camera

Events

Speakers, crowds and moments that only happen once. I shoot TEDx Samaru and community events, and plan positions before the day so I never block the audience.

Portraits and documentary

Campus life and the people around Zaria. Natural light where possible; the goal is a photo that tells you something about the person.
