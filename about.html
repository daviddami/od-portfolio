About me

Olokoba David Oluwadamilola

Who I am, how I got here, and how I like to work.

The short version

I’m a final-year Computer Engineering student at Ahmadu Bello University in Zaria, Kaduna State, Nigeria. I’m detail-oriented, with a multidisciplinary background in technology, creative media and community engagement. Alongside my degree I’ve built practical skills in programming, IT support and hardware troubleshooting, with hands-on experience in Python and network support.

How I work

Most of what I know about delivering work came from events. As Head of Technical Unit at TEDx Samaru I plan the equipment, run the checks and coordinate a volunteer crew so the talk starts on time whatever happens backstage. As Lead Photographer I use visual storytelling to make that work visible afterwards.

My internship at PTDF in Abuja added the other side: collecting and analysing data, preparing reports and presentations for stakeholders, and keeping a team’s schedule and communication on track.

What I value

  • A positive attitude. Problems get solved faster by people who expect them to be solvable.
  • Teamwork. Every event I’ve worked on succeeded because the crew trusted each other.
  • Being organised and efficient. Checklists, clear roles and early setup beat heroics on the day.
  • Community. Volunteering with TEDx Samaru, Enactus ABU and KARFART is where I’ve grown most.
